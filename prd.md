# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Project Overview
**Project Name:** Ankita Enterprises & Ankita Furniture Work Web App
**Objective:** To build a luxurious, modern, and premium web application that serves as a digital portfolio and lead-generation tool. The platform will showcase the physical craftsmanship, turnkey project execution, and interior design expertise of the business.

## 2. Target Audience
High-end residential and commercial clients seeking:
*   Complete turnkey project execution (from bare shell to fully furnished).
*   Bespoke, custom-built furniture.
*   Physical, hands-on interior design services.

## 3. Key Features & Requirements
*   **Hero Section:** High-impact visual landing with a clear value proposition and call-to-action (CTA).
*   **About Section:** Brand story highlighting 15+ years of experience and commitment to quality.
*   **Services Section:** Clear breakdown of core offerings: Turnkey Projects, Custom Furniture, and Interior Design.
*   **Portfolio / Gallery:** A visually stunning grid layout to showcase high-quality before-and-after photos or completed project images.
*   **Contact / Lead Generation:** A functional contact form to capture client inquiries, along with direct contact details (Email, Phone, Location).
*   **Responsive Design:** Must look and function perfectly on mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.

## 4. Non-Goals (Out of Scope)
*   **No E-commerce:** The site will not process online sales or have a shopping cart.
*   **No Virtual Design Tools:** Emphasize physical craftsmanship; no 3D modeling tools for users.

## 5. Design & Aesthetic Guidelines
*   **Vibe:** Luxurious, premium, professional, and modern.
*   **Color Palette:** Deep Charcoal/Midnight Blue (Primary), Warm Gold (Accent), Alabaster/Off-white (Background).
*   **Typography:** Elegant serif headings (`Playfair Display`) paired with clean sans-serif body text (`Inter`).
